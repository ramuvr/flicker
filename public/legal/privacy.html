2. Summary

  • We cannot read what you share. Your text is encrypted in your browser before it is sent, and the key never reaches our server.
  • There are no accounts, logins, cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party scripts.
  • The encrypted text is kept in server memory only, never written to disk, and is deleted the first time the link is opened or when it expires, whichever comes first.
  • Your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to stop abuse, and is discarded within about two minutes.
  • A bot check runs in your browser when you create a secret. It is a short calculation, not a puzzle, and it collects no personal data.

3. How the service works

When you create a secret, your browser generates a random 256-bit key and encrypts your text with AES-256-GCM. Only the encrypted result is uploaded. The key is placed in the part of the link after the # symbol, which browsers do not send to web servers. When the recipient opens the link and selects Reveal, the server hands over the encrypted text and deletes it in the same step, and the recipient's browser decrypts it.

4. Information we process

Full details, including how long each item is kept, are in the Data Collection Policy. In short:

  • Encrypted secret, random link ID and expiry time: needed to provide the service. We cannot decrypt the secret.
  • IP address (IPv6: network prefix) and a request count: used only for rate limiting to protect the service from abuse.
  • Bot check result: when you create a secret, your browser solves a small proof-of-work challenge (using the open-source ALTCHA software, run on our own server). We keep only the random ID of the solved challenge, for up to 10 minutes, so it cannot be reused. The check does not track mouse movements or keystrokes, set cookies or contact any third party.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device fingerprints, location or browsing history, and we do not use cookies or browser storage.

2. Data the service never receives or stores

  • Your secret in readable form, or the decryption key (the part of the link after #).
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, accounts or passwords.
  • Cookies, local storage, tracking pixels, analytics or advertising identifiers.
  • Third-party scripts, fonts or embeds that could collect data about you.
  • Application request logs.
  • Mouse movements, keystrokes or other behavioural data. The bot check is a calculation your browser performs; its interaction tracking feature is switched off.

3. Bot check

When you create a secret, your browser downloads a signed challenge from our server and spends a second or two solving it in the background (ALTCHA proof-of-work). The answer is sent with your encrypted secret and checked by our server. This makes it slow and costly for automated software to create secrets in bulk. All ALTCHA code is served from our own server; no data goes to ALTCHA or any other third party.

4. Data in your browser

Your text and the key exist in your browser's memory while the page is open. The key is removed from the address bar and history as soon as a link is opened. If you copy a secret or link to your clipboard, it stays there until you replace it; the service cannot clear it for you.

5. Deletion

  • Automatic: on first view, on expiry, or when the server restarts.
  • Manual: open your own link and select Reveal to destroy it before the recipient does.
  • Expired items are removed from memory within about one minute of expiry and are never returned after expiry.
